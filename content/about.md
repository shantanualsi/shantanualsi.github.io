I'm Shantanu Alshi. I work on distributed systems and spend a lot of my time
on the parts of engineering that don't show up in a diff — how teams decide,
how systems fail, and what actually moves the needle.

This blog is where I think out loud about both: the mechanics of building
reliable systems, and what changes about the job as your biggest lever stops
being the code you write.
